Inviting mints a single-use key and emails it. The key works once, on one device; somebody who reinstalls needs a new one.
Actions are immediate and use the same code path as the in-app moderation tools. Hide and suspend are reversible; decide closes the report.

A post on the shared plane, by id
Say what you would do with a post, and for each category the machine raised, whether it is really there. Fiction counts as fiction: a villain's threat is not harassment, a battle is not graphic violence unless it is.
A rule fires without a person, so what matters is precision: when the score is high, did you agree? Recall is among the machine's own candidates only. Standing is decided on confirmed cases, never on a perfect record over three posts.
Each line is the score at or above which that action applies; blank means never. Fold is a cover for everybody. Review is a cover plus a queue entry. Escalate jumps the queue and tells the admin now; hold also hides the post until a person restores it. Saving re-decides every stored score under the new lines.
